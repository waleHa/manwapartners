What we do

From creator search to campaign learning.

We manage the parts that make creator partnerships work - finding the right people, shaping the campaign, coordinating the details, and learning from the results.

01

Influencer sourcing

Find YouTube creators in smartphones, wearables, mobile audio, and accessories based on audience fit, recent views, content format, geography, and product relevance.

02

Outreach & negotiation

Handle creator outreach, rates, deliverables, usage rights, exclusivity, timing, and communication from first contact to clear agreement.

03

Campaign management

Coordinate briefs, product shipment timing, approvals, deliverables, creator communication, and publication around launches or evergreen campaigns.

04

Content strategy

Choose the right format for the product: reviews, comparisons, camera or battery tests, long-term use, buying guides, demonstrations, and launch coverage.

05

Reporting & learning

Turn results into better decisions about creators, formats, launch timing, audience segments, product angles, and the partnerships worth repeating.
